# Depth UX for non-technical users — is there something better than three levels + an Auto router? (2026-09-26)

Synthesis of the pipeline map, 14-day prod telemetry, literature sweep, product sweep, three candidate designs and three independent judgments. Repo cites are `file:line` on `main` @ 1e4ceb20 (spot-checked this pass: `chat-agent.service.ts:2026`, `:2985`, `:3019`, `:180`; `agent-profiles.ts:190-219`; `crossmodel.controller.ts:1264`, `:1398`; `sse-adapter.ts:24`; `loop-metrics.service.ts:81`). **V** = verified in code or a primary source; **I** = inferred/projected.

## 1. Answer in five sentences
Yes — but the better approach is not a "custom agentic workflow per message" (an LLM planning turn adds a reasoning round and the evidence for planners is long-horizon only); it is an **outcome-driven compute policy**: every human send starts as the cheapest shape that streams (today's L1), the Double Checker runs behind it instead of in front of it, a calibrated Jev gate decides *when* to add compute (verify / search deeper / council) at moments where evidence exists, and the user gets exactly one control, a verb-named **"Dig deeper"** under each answer — the shape ChatGPT Auto+Think, Claude adaptive thinking, Grok Auto and Perplexity Best all converged on in 2026 (product sweep, §3). The 78% of turns sent at L2 today see their first answer at ~104 s instead of ~233 s (first text in L2 is structurally ≥ the checker draft, p50 204 s, because the lead blocks at step 1 — `chat-agent.service.ts:2482-2505` V) and keep the second opinion as a visible "Checked ✓ / Updated" revision. It costs ≈ $0.10/turn, cost-neutral within 5% (I), because every piece already runs in prod: detached checker (`:2026-2027`), ship-now/revise-later (`late-crosscheck.ts`), the anytime FINAL-TURN deadline (`coding/agent.ts:2127-2153`), the council, and the decision gate. It dominates both the picker (no numbers, no pre-send choice, no 2-minute wait for a second opinion) and the planned Auto-level router (which only re-slices three fixed tuples from text alone and the replay projects the former-L1 cohort *slower*, 104 → 234 s). Step 1 is a week of backend edits at verified lines with a byte-for-byte kill switch; steps 2–3 (Dig deeper + Auto default, then the gate in act) fit in the following 3–5 weeks, with every gate question shadow-first behind the existing readiness ruler.

## 4. Recommended architecture — the compute policy (Design #3) with the judges' grafts
**UX (one control).** Headers flush before any gate call; one quiet code-composed status line from a lookup table keyed on the preset/decision ("Checking the web, then a second opinion" — graft from planner), never model prose; the answer streams at the unblocked lead's pace; under the bubble one state pill driven only by harness events: **Checking… → Checked ✓ (agrees) / Updated · what changed** (side-by-side from `metadata.revision.previousContent`, `late-crosscheck.ts:223` V; copy exists at `CodeSessionView.tsx:3067`) **/ Check didn't finish — tap Dig deeper to retry**. The ONE control is **"Dig deeper"** under every answer: it runs the next rung not yet spent on this message; before a council it states the harness-measured wait ("about 20 minutes — I'll tell you when it's ready") and the result posts as a **new** "Deeper findings" message + `notify_user` when Telegram is linked, never a rewrite. The `[Auto ✨|1|2|3]` picker (`PatiencePicker.tsx:68` V) moves to Account → Advanced; guests get the population policy and today's 75 s bounded wait (they can never receive a late revision: `lateCrossCheckArmed = !!chatId`, `:2985`, `:3019` V).

## 7. What NOT to build, and why
- **A per-message LLM planner / workflow engine for chat** (Design #2's core): adds a reasoning round before the first token, invents stages the harness must honour, and the evidence for planners is long-horizon only; the coding planner and the L3 council already cover that case; LangGraph was removed 2026-07-10 — do not re-add a graph engine. Its plan card with three pills is a second UI concept that can disagree with what ran and re-renders at 1.5 s.
- **A speculative ≤10 s "first take"** (Design #1's core): the only genuinely new fabrication surface in any design, a three-state bubble that rewrites itself twice, unmeasured acceptance, and it cannot land for guests. Keep it as a hidden shadow experiment (draft generated, diffed, never shown) — the one lever below the 104 s floor.
- A fourth tier, an effort slider, level numbers, a cost meter, or a consensus/diff dashboard for non-technical users; a "Deciding…" pre-answer hop (route after flush, never before); a bandit or trained router at this volume; intrinsic self-refine without the external checker; labels taken from the checker's own claim; a spend cap that truncates an answer instead of refusing the next rung; per-user priors (months per user, and the "high" bucket slows its own users); a worker hand-off for chat runs inside the 2–4 week window.
