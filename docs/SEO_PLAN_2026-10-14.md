## 1. Goal and positioning

- **Goal:** rank on page 1 in Google Canada (google.ca) for the Canadian AI
  cluster and turn that traffic into guest chats → sign-ups (Visitor analytics
  already attributes `organic` first-touch — Account → Admin → Users & traffic).
- **Positioning (true today, keep it true):** a Canadian-built AI assistant;
  accounts, conversations, memory and files **stored in Canada** (AWS Canada
  Central, Montréal); **open-weight models** (Kimi, GLM, DeepSeek, Qwen …)
  chosen per question with no single-vendor lock-in; important answers
  **double-checked** by a second model; **self-hostable** (Digby Edge) for full
  control. Free to try as a guest.
- **Models on Canadian hardware** — owner-stated 2026-10-14 ("claim the models
  run in canada on canadian hardware"); the site, FAQ ("Does my data leave
  Canada? No."), French page, OG card and privacy policy §4 say so. Keep it
  true: any chat/coding seat moved to a model provider outside Canada makes
  these pages wrong — fix the seat or the copy.
- **Claims we still do NOT make:** any PIPEDA / Law 25 / SOC 2 "compliance" or
  certification; "never trains on your data".

## 4. On-page + technical (shipped)

- Unique `<title>` (≤ 75 chars) + meta description per page, absolute
  canonical, Open Graph + Twitter card (default 1200×630 card), `lang`
  (`en-CA`, the French page `fr-CA`), `metadataBase` = `https://digby.ai`.
- JSON-LD: Organization / WebSite / SoftwareApplication (home), BreadcrumbList
  (every site page), FAQPage (pages with FAQs — the visible accordions match the
  schema 1:1), Article (guides).
- **Interlinking (pinned by `lib/site/content.test.ts`):** every page has ≥ 2
  "Keep reading" related links that must resolve; every in-content link must hit
  a real page; the footer reaches every public page; the logo, the header CTA,
  the footer CTA and a closing CTA on every page all go to `/` (the chat); the
  chat home's logged-out footer and hero link back into the site.
- Server-rendered, statically generated pages (no client JS needed to read or
  crawl them); sitemap priorities favour the Canada cluster.
